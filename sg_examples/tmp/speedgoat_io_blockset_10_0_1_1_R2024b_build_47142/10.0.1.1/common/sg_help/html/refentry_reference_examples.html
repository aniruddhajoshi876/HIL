Introduction

Start your application with fully assembled models, ready-to-run on Speedgoat real-time hardware. Reference example models are customizable and extendable, allowing you to incorporate your own subsystems and change them as needed. They are distributed as Simulink® projects and come with MATLAB® Live Scripts, providing detailed documentation and step-by-step instructions to successfully run the examples.

For comprehensive information about our reference examples, including demonstration videos and detailed test system configurations, visit the pages below in our Knowledge Center.
