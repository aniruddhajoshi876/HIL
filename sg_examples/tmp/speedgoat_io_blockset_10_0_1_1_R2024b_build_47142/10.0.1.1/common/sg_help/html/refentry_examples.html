Speedgoat Product and Reference Examples

Product Examples

Product Examples introduce you to the fundamentals of specific Speedgoat products and will get you up and running with your Speedgoat test system in no time at all. Covering a range of I/O connectivity and communication protocols, these examples will guide you in using Speedgoat's driver blocks within Simulink® to effectively implement and manage the available interfaces.

Reference Examples

Successfully implement your specific use-case application with fully assembled models, ready-to-run on Speedgoat real-time hardware. Reference example models are designed for industry-specific testing applications and are both customizable and extendable, allowing you to incorporate your own subsystems and change them as needed. They are distributed as Simulink projects and come with MATLAB® Live Scripts, providing detailed documentation and step-by-step instructions ensuring you can successfully run and adapt the examples to your own requirements.
