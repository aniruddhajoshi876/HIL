Software Support Information — Software compatibility with MATLAB releases

Compatibility Changes as of MATLAB R2026a

With the introduction of MATLAB R2026a and Antelope RTOS™, software support for certain earlier-generation products ends as of R2026a. Systems that include the products listed below continue to operate with MATLAB releases up to and including R2025b.

What this Means for You

If you continue using MATLAB releases up to and including R2025b, no immediate action is required. Your current setups remain unaffected and fully operational.

  • Your systems continue to work with MATLAB releases up to and including R2025b

  • Software maintenance, technical support, and bug fixes for the listed products will continue until at least September 2028

  • Your hardware warranty and service coverage remain unchanged according to your agreement

  • Many products remain available for purchase for R2025b and earlier MATLAB versions

Planning to Upgrade to R2026a

If your system includes one of the products listed below, we offer successor or alternative solutions with long-term availability. In this case, please contact our Sales team, who will be happy to assist you in planning a smooth transition.

More information about the changes introduced in MATLAB R2026a is available here: MATLAB R2026a Major Update.
