Speedgoat Customer Portal

The Speedgoat Customer Portal allows you to:

  • Download software and documentation for your Speedgoat hardware

  • Check the status of your warranty and service packages

If you do not yet have an account and you are an existing customer, getting yourself up and running is as easy as 1, 2, 3:

  1. In your web browser, go to speedgoat.com/newaccount

  2. Fill out the Create Account form (all text fields are required)

  3. Once you verify your email, you will receive an email to set your password

Please contact support@speedgoat.com in case of problems.
