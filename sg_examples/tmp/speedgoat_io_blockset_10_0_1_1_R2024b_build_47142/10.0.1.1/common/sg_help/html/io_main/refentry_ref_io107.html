IO107

IO107 — Fast 16-bit analog module with 16 differential analog output channels

Introduction

The IO107 I/O module provides 16 differential output channels and a resolution of 16 bits. Each of the 16 output channels has its dedicated D/A converter with an update time of 2.2μs.

Warning

This I/O module has an automatic calibration feature. During the calibration process various signals may be present at the output pins.
