Analog IO337-650k

Analog IO337-650k — 32 x 16-bit DACs offering an update rate of up to 10 MSPS, 8 x 16-bit ADCs offering a sample rate of up to 5 MSPS

Introduction

The IO337-650k I/O module provides the following analog functionality:

  • 32 x 16-bit DACs offering an update rate of up to 10 MSPS

  • 8 x 16-bit ADCs offering a sample rate (with simultaneous sampling) of up to 5 MSPS
