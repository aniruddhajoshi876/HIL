IO101

IO101 — 16-bit analog module with 32/16 sequential analog inputs, 8 analog outputs, and 16 digital TTL channel

Introduction

The IO101 is a 16-bit analog module with 32 single-ended, or 16 differential analog inputs, 8 analog outputs, and 16 digital TTL I/O lines configurable as inputs or outputs in two pairs of 8.
