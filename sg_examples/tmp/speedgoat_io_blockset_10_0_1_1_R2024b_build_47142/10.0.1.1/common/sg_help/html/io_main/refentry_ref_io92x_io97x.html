IO92X, IO970-IO973

IO92X, IO970-IO973 — The IO92X and IO970-IO973 are programmable resistor I/O modules

Introduction

The IO92x and IO970-IO973 series is a range of programmable resistor modules.

They all have a common driver block which is described in the 'Programmable Resistor Control block' section.
