IO183 — Fast 16-bit analog input and output module

Introduction

The IO183 is a 16-bit analog module with either 8 single-ended or 4 differential analog inputs, 4 analog outputs and 16 digital TTL I/O lines configurable as inputs or outputs

  • 8/4 analog input channels featuring:

    • Single-ended (8) or differential coupling (4)

    • Voltage range of ±5 V, ±10 V, 0–5 V and 0–10 V (applies to all inputs)

  • 4 analog output channels featuring:

    • Voltage range of 0–2.5 V and 0–5 V (applies to all outputs)

  • 16 digital I/O channels
