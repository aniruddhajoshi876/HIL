IO102

IO102 — Fast 16-bit analog module with 32/16 multiplexed analog input channels and 4 analog output channels, and 16 digital TTL channels

Introduction

The IO102 is a fast 16-bit analog module with 32 single-ended or 16 differential analog input, 4 analog output and 16 digital I/O TTL channels.

Warning

This I/O module has an automatic calibration feature. During the calibration process various signals may be present at the output pins.
