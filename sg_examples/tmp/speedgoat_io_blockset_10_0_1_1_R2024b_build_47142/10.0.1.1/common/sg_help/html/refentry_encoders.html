Encoders

Encoders — Speedgoat support for encoders

Introduction

Speedgoat offers support for reading and emulating most encoders available on the market today. Support is implemented using code modules that are used by either Configurable I/O modules or Simulink-programmable FPGA I/O modules.

Code Modules for Encoders

BiSS Encoder, decoder and sniffer
Cam and Crank Encoder and decoder
EnDat Encoder, decoder and sniffer
SSI Encoder, decoder and sniffer
Quadrature Encoder and decoder
