Resistors — Speedgoat I/O modules for resistor simulation

Introduction

Speedgoat offers a range of programmable resistor modules with up to 18 channels of 8-bit resolution resistor chains in a single PCI slot. The flexible architecture allows the card to also be supplied in 12-bit, 16-bit or 24-bit resolution versions, for applications requiring finer resolution, a greater resistance range, or a higher channel count.
