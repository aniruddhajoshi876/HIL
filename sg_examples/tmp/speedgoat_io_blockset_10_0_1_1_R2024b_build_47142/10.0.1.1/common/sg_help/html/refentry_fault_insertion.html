Introduction

Speedgoat provide a comprehensive range of fault insertion I/O modules. These modules are specifically designed for safety critical applications, often in aerospace or automotive industries, where the behavior of a control system in response to unusual sensor conditions must be fully evaluated. Typically they would be used in hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing to apply sensor conditions which could occur because of corrosion, short/open circuits and other electrical failures inherited through age, damage or faulty installation. The modules greatly simplify and accelerate this type of testing.
