IO50X/IO581

IO50X/IO581 — The IO50X/IO581 I/O modules are serial modules supporting RS232/422/485 protocols

Please note that the actual achievable baud rate depends on the oscillator frequency of the hardware. The selected baud rates must be a divisor of the oscillator frequency. Note that the divisor must be an integer for the I/O modules IO503,IO504 and IO505.
