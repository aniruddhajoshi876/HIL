IO104

IO104 — Very fast 16-bit analog module with 8 simultaneous sampling analog input channels, 4 analog output channels, 16 digital TTL channels

Introduction

The IO104 is a very fast 16-bit analog module with 8 single-ended or 8 differential simultaneous sampling analog inputs, 4 analog single-ended simultaneous outputs and two 8-bit digital inputs or output ports.

The driver version 2 enables synchronized, frame based and triggered input and output using two DMA channels.

Warning

This I/O module has an automatic calibration feature. During the calibration process various signals may be present at the output pins.
