Analog — Analog-to-digital (ADC) and digital-to-analog (DAC)

Introduction

Analog functionality for both analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog converters are provided with a wide range of dedicated configurable I/O modules or I/O interface extensions.

IO33x-06 16 x 16-bit 500kHz ADs, 8 x 16-bit DAs with 10us settling time, 16 x digital TTL I/O line
IO33x-07 16 x 16-bit analog outputs with 2us settling time, +/- 10V
IO33x-08 8 x 16-bit analog outputs with 1.4us settling time, +/- 10V
IO32x 32 x 16-bit 125kHz to 1MHz ADs, 8 x 16-bit DAs with 10us settling time
IO324-200k 32 x 16-bit 125kHz to 1MHz ADs, 8 x 16-bit DAs with <1us settling time
IO325-160k 8 x 16-bit ADCs up to 1.5 MSPS sampling rate, 4 x 16-bit DACs up to 10 MSPS update rate
IO334-325k 16 x 16-bit ADCs up to 5 MSPS sampling rate, 16 x 16-bit DACs up to 10 MSPS update rate
IO336-325k 16 x 16-bit ADCs up to 1.5 MSPS sampling rate, 8 x 16-bit DACs up to 10 MSPS update rate
IO337-650k 32 x 16-bit DACs offering an update rate of up to 10 MSPS, 8 x 16-bit ADCs offering a sample rate of up to 5 MSPS
IO397 4 x 16-bit ADs with 200ksps, 4 x 16-bit DAs with simultaneous sampling, 14 x digital TTL I/O lines
