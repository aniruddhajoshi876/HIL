IO611

IO611 — Intelligent CAN module with 2x CAN channels configurable as CAN FD, high-speed CAN or low-speed CAN, and 2x LIN channels

Introduction

The IO611 offers two CAN channels. Each channel can be configured individually to one of the following three operating modes:

  • CAN FD

  • High-speed CAN

  • Low-speed CAN

In addition, there are two LIN channels.

The channels are located on two DB9 - connectors. For a description of the pinout, refer to the I/O pin mapping section.
