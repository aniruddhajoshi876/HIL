Note

Since resolver, LVDTs, and synchros are floating devices, the Ground pins shown are not strictly necessary, but could be of use when the module is used with non-floating sensors or ground referenced equipment. Unused inputs can be left open.

Applications that use the excitation outputs must connect these externally to the excitation inputs.

The excitation outputs do not float, but are ground referenced. The EXC_OUT+/- signals are each positive voltages in reference to ground.
