IO292

IO292 — Digital I/O module with 24 I/O lines.

Introduction

The IO292 I/O module is a digital module. It provides a total of 24 digital I/O channels and supports a 24 mA source/sink current. The module comprises three banks of eight I/O channels: bank A: I/O 01-08, bank B: I/O 09-16, and bank C: I/O 17-24. Each bank can be programmed as input or output, and bank C can be further separated into two groups of four I/O lines.
