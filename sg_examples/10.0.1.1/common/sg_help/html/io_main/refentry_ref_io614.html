IO614

IO614 — Intelligent CAN module with 4x CAN HS channels (channel 1 can be LS instead) and 1x LIN channel

Introduction

The IO614 offers four CAN HS channels.

Channel 1 can be configured as CAN LS instead.

Channel 2 contains an additional LIN channel.

The channels are located on four DB9 - connectors. For a descripton of the pinout, refer to the I/O pin mapping section.
