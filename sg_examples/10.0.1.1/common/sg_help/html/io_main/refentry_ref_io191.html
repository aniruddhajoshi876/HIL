IO191

IO191 — Fast 16-bit analog input and output module

Introduction

The IO191 I/O module is a 16-bit analog module with either 8 single-ended or 4 differential analog inputs (maximum sample rate of 250 kSPS depending on the number of active channels), 4 single-ended analog outputs, and 16 digital I/O lines configurable as inputs or outputs, 8 of which can be individually software-selected as input or output.

The voltage ranges (software-selectable by channel) are as follows:

  • Analog input channels:

    • Single-ended: ±0.64 V, ±1.28 V, ±2.56 V, ±5.12 V, ±10.24 V

    • Differential: ±0.64 V, ±1.28 V, ±2.56 V, ±5.12 V, ±10.24 V, ±20.48 V and ±24.576 V

  • Analog output channels:

    • 0–5 V, 0–10 V, ±2.5 V, ±5 V and ±10 V (software-selectable per channel)
