For proper operation, a CAN topology requires electrical termination in the form of resistors.

The IO691 I/O module is delivered with the termination resistors disabled. The termination resistors can be enabled by request at the time of order.

Channel Location

The chassis of the target machine has two DB9 connectors labelled 'A' and 'B'. In this documentation, channels 1 and 2 are used to differentiate between the available channels. The mapping of the channels to the connectors is shown in the following table.

Channel Connector
1 A
