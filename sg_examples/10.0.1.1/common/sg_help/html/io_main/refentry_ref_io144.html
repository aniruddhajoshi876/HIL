IO144

IO144 — Fast simultaneous sampling 16-bit analog voltage/current output module

Introduction

The IO144 I/O module is a 16-bit analog module with 16 analog outputs and 20 digital TTL I/O lines configurable as inputs or outputs.

  • 16 analog output channels featuring:

    • Voltage ranges of 0–5 V, 0–10 V, 0–12 V, ±5 V, ±10 V, ±12 V (individual setting available for each channel)

      Note

      The 0–12 V and ±12 V ranges indicate the over-range capability of the analog output channels.

    • Current ranges of 4–20 mA, 0–20 mA, 0–24 mA (individual setting available for each channel)

  • 20 digital I/O channels
