Battery Management Testing Systems — Battery Management Testing Systems (BCE, FIU, TSE)

Introduction

The Speedgoat Battery Management Testing Systems (BMTS) denote a modular family of devices for the emulation and measurement of batteries for rapid control prototyping and hardware in-the-loop testing.

The Battery Cell Emulator (BCE) enables 12 independent and isolated battery cells to be emulated in one single unit. The stackable design allows hundreds of cells to be simulated in order to reproduce battery packs and fully test battery management systems (BMS).

The Fault Insertion Unit (FIU) enables the injection of faults, such as broken wire, short circuit or polarity inversion, in up to 36 channels per unit.

The Temperature Sensor Emulation (TSE) unit enables the emulation of up to 36 resistive temperature sensors per unit.
