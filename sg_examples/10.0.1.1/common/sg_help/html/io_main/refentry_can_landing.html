CAN FD, CAN, J1939 and LIN — Speedgoat I/O modules for CAN FD, CAN, J1939 and LIN

Introduction

CAN FD, CAN, J1939 and LIN are communication standards widely used in automotive and many other industries.

Speedgoat provides a range of I/O modules for communicating with devices supporting these protocols, all of which come with Simulink driver blocks.

I/O modules

IO601 Intelligent CAN module with 2 isolated, software-selectable high/low speed CAN channels
IO602Intelligent CAN module with 2x or 4x CAN channels configurable as CAN FD or high-speed CAN
IO603Intelligent CAN module with 4x CAN channels configurable as CAN FD or high-speed CAN, 2x LIN channels configurable as Master or Slave and 4 GPIO channels
IO610Intelligent CAN module with 2x CAN channels configurable as CAN FD or high-speed CAN
IO611 Intelligent CAN module with 2x CAN channels configurable as CAN FD, high-speed CAN or low-speed CAN, and 2x LIN channels
IO612 Intelligent CAN module with 4 HS channels
IO613 Intelligent CAN module with 2x CAN channels configurable as CAN FD or high-speed CAN
IO614 Intelligent CAN module with 4x CAN HS channels (channel 1 can be LS instead) and 1x LIN channel
