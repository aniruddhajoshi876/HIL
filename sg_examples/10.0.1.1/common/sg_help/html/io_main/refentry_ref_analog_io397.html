Analog IO397

Analog IO397 — 4 x 16-bit ADs with 200ksps, 4 x 16-bit DAs with simultaneous sampling, 14 x digital TTL I/O lines

Introduction

The IO397 I/O module provides following analog functionality:

14 x ESD protected 5V TTL I/O with pull resistors. Voltage levels of 3.3V, 5V, or ground, and input/output direction are software configurable by I/O line

4 x 16-bit analog inputs, 200 ksps, simultaneous sampling differential/single-ended ADC

4 x 16-bit analog outputs with simultaneous sampling and software selectable ranges: 0-5 V, 0-10 V, 0-10.8 V, ±5 V, ±10 V, ±10.8 V
