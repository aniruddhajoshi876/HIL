Digital I/O — Speedgoat digital I/O modules

Introduction

Speedgoat offers a range of digital I/O modules, differentiated by the number of available channels, type of signal levels, isolation, and voltage/current levels.

Digital I/O

IO203 The IO203 is a digital I/O module providing a total of 64 TTL channels.
IO204 The IO204 is a digital I/O module providing 16x 24V inputs and 16x 24V/0.5A outputs.
IO205 The IO205 is a digital I/O module providing 32 digital 24V/0.5A outputs; high side switches.
IO206 The IO206 is a digital I/O module providing 32 digital inputs
IO207 The IO207 I/O module provides a total of 128 digital TTL lines.
IO290 Digital I/O module with isolated inputs, solid state FET outputs and LVTTL I/O lines.
IO291 Digital I/O module with isolated inputs, solid state FET outputs and LVTTL I/O lines.
IO292 Digital I/O module with 24 I/O lines.
