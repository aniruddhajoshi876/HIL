IO103

IO103 — Fast 16-bit analog module with 16/8 multiplexed analog input channels, 8 analog output channels and 4 digital TTL output

Introduction

The IO103 is a fast 16-bit analog module with 16 single-ended, or 8 differential analog inputs, and 8 single-ended analog outputs.

Warning

This I/O module has an automatic calibration feature. During the calibration process various signals may be present at the output pins.
