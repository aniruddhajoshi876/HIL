IO113

IO113 — 18-bit analog module with 8 single-ended or 3-wire differential output channels

Introduction

This I/O module provides 8 single-ended or 8 differential 18-bit analog outputs and 8 bidirectional digital TTL I/O lines.

Warning

This I/O module has an automatic calibration feature. During the calibration process various signals may be present at the output pins.
