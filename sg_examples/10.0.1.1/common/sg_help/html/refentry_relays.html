Relays — Speedgoat support for relays

Introduction

Speedgoat offers a range of relay I/O modules with SPST, DPST and SPDT configurations. For low signal levels, use I/O modules with instrument grade Ruthenium reed relays with a low thermal offset voltage to ensure accurate operation under all conditions and a long service life. For higher voltages and currents, use I/O modules with electro-mechanical relays.
